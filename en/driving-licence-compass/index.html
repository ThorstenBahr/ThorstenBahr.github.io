Your path to the driving licence – faster and cheaper

The Driving Licence Compass (FührerscheinKompass) is the hands-on guide for class B and BF17 in Germany: plan your training like a project, avoid unnecessary spending and walk into every exam prepared. I'm sharing all the experience and knowledge from my time as a self-employed driving instructor. Currently available in German.

What's inside

Not an off-the-shelf guide

The Compass combines step-by-step planning with knowledge you won't read anywhere else: how driving schools calculate, what examiners really ask – and how to stay calm when it counts.

The 5-phase method

From planning 6–12 months before enrolment to being test-ready – every step at the right time, nothing left to chance.

Insider cost knowledge

Understand how a driving school operates as a business – and why well-prepared learners get appointments faster and reach their licence at lower cost.

Vehicle tech module

Tyres, fluids, pre-drive checks: the technical questions of the practical test – explained with the why, what for and what if.

Controls checklist

22 blocks to tick off: get to know the car at home before the first paid driving lesson – from light switch to engine bay.

Deadlines & paperwork

Eye test, first-aid course, licence application, BF17 documents and all exam deadlines – so nothing expires and nothing is paid twice.

Mindset & test anxiety

Five proven techniques against test anxiety – from paced breathing to the dress rehearsal, each with instructions and the reason it works.
