Privacy policy

2. General information

The protection of your personal data is important to me. I process personal data only to the extent necessary and on the basis of the applicable data protection laws (GDPR, BDSG).

3. Hosting and server log files

When you access this website, the hosting provider automatically stores information in server log files (e.g. IP address, date and time, page accessed, browser type) for technical provision and security. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR. The specific hosting provider will be named here before go-live.

4. Contact (form, e-mail, phone)

If you contact me via the contact form, by e-mail or by phone, I process your details to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1)(b) or (f) GDPR. The data is deleted once it is no longer required and no statutory retention obligations apply. A service provider (e.g. Formspree) may be used for the technical delivery of the form; details will be added here.

5. No tracking cookies, no analytics

This website uses no tracking cookies and integrates no analytics or advertising services. No profiling takes place.

6. Fonts (self-hosted)

The fonts used are served exclusively locally from this server. There isno connection to Google Fonts or other external font services – no data is transmitted to third parties for this purpose.

7. External links and social media

This website links to external profiles (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook). Data is only transmitted to the respective providers when you click these links; their privacy policies then apply.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have in particular the following rights:

  • Access to your stored data (Art. 15)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • Erasure (Art. 17) and restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability (Art. 20)
  • Objection to processing (Art. 21)
  • Complaint to a supervisory authority (Art. 77) – the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection is competent

9. Changes to this policy

I will update this privacy policy as soon as changes to the website or the legal framework require it.
