About me

Personal. Free.
By your side.

I am Thorsten Bahr – a free insurance broker from Central Hesse. I protect your wealth against risks and help you build real value with property – freely, honestly and provider-neutral.

Thorsten Bahr · Your personal contact
My path

Advice is a matter of trust

For years I have supported people through the key financial decisions of their lives – from their first insurance cover through family planning to retirement provision. One thing has become clear: good advice does not come from a product, but from truly understanding a person's situation and goals.

That is exactly why I took the step into free brokerage. As a free broker I am committed to no one but you – I compare the market and look for the solution that fits your life.
